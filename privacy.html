1. Introduction

Welcome to HEICX. We provide a browser-based tool that allows users to convert HEIC and HEIF images into JPG files.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep the service simple, transparent and secure.

2. Your Images

HEICX is designed to process your images directly in your web browser.

When the converter is working normally, the image files you select are processed locally on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to an HEICX server.

Your original image files are not stored by HEICX as part of the normal conversion process.

6. HEIC Conversion Library

HEICX uses a client-side conversion library to process supported HEIC and HEIF images in the browser.

The conversion process is designed to take place on the user's device rather than requiring the image to be uploaded to a conversion server.
